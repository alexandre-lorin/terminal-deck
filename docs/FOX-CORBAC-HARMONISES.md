# Fox Loaf and Corbac mini — harmonized versions

Retouch requested by Toubi on 25 October 2026: the first drawings were too detailed compared to Blob, Vesper and Byte. The colors of the fox and the crow remain recognizable; contours, eyes and shadow areas are simplified to get closer to the trio.

Creation and retouching carried out with the **imagegen** skill, an integrated tool. `trio.png` serves as a style reference; the first two atlases serve as targets. Two intermediate versions `*-sheet-v2.png` and the initial versions remain retained. No source images have been overwritten.

## Active assets

- `assets/mascots/fox-loaf-sheet-v3.png` — 1024 × 1536, RGBA, 24 poses.
- `assets/mascots/corbac-mini-sheet-v3.png` — 1024 × 1536, RGBA, 24 poses.

The catalog keeps the indices 3 and 4: the saved companion choices remain valid. Rendering, quotas and workbench use the same new sources. Old drafts are not silently applied to new artboards: keys include the source file name.

## Framing and display

Four columns and six sequences: curiosity, keyboard, wait, hello, yawn/sleep, wake up. The windows remain 256 × 256 px. The vertical origins actually inspected are:

- Fox Loaf: 16, 256, 496, 736, 976, 1224 px.
- Corbac mini: 16, 256, 496, 736, 976, 1216 px.

A mask of 16 px source on the left of the fox cells excludes a small spurious fragment in one margin; it doesn't cut into her figure. The same mask is taken into account in the display and in the workshop exports, where it is adjustable. The generated PNGs remain unchanged. A visual enlargement of 1,3 in Deck compensates for the wider margins and brings their apparent size closer to that of the trio. The exports remain source poses, without Deck's CSS transformations.

These images are generated,with a coarser pixel aesthetic; the grid prompt 32 × 32 is a visual goal, not a certification of manual drawing or exact palette.
