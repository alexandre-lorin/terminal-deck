## Taste, interface and usage experience of Toubi

- The pleasure of use and good taste are part of the expected result, at the same time
  title as good functioning. Seek a coherent identity and atmosphere
  pleasant, with interactions that make you want to come back to the tool.
- In a construction or improvement already authorized, take initiatives
  creative and offer unique details when they serve the purpose. Don't
  extend this freedom to deletions, migrations or architectural changes.
- Prefer deep black, sober surfaces, clean typography, lines
  fine and a clear hierarchy. The colored accents remain discreet; preserve
  a neutral option when requested.
- Firefox local references Nord Violet and Perigee, under
  `/home/toubi/01 - Dev/00 - Projets à tester/html-css--firefox-nord-violet` and
  `/home/toubi/.config/firefox-nord-violet`, help to understand this taste. Consult them
  read-only if useful, without modifying Firefox to work on another tool.
- Give space to the content: remove repeated information, counters
  decorative, large introductions and commands that clutter the screen.
  Make secondary actions available on demand.
- Take particular care of the keyboard, collage, focus, resumption of work
  and context changes. Respect agreed shortcuts and modes
  native input of hosted applications.
- Check the behavior with the battery actually used: a simulation
  or a test that replaces a component does not prove that that component works.
  Distinguish between what has been verified, what remains to be tested and the known limits.
- Move forward with useful and consistent improvements. A visual personality should not
  not hide real states, invent statuses or complicate a simple action.
